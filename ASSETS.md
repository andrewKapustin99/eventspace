# Иллюстративные фотографии

Фотографии используются для вымышленных карточек и не представляют реальные предложения в Минске.

## Созданный актив

`dist/assets/orangerie.jpg` — встроенный ImageGen, photorealistic-natural. Исходник сохранён в рабочей папке generated_images. Итоговый prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide 16:9 website hero photograph, fictional event venue listing. Primary request: premium editorial architectural photograph of a beautiful European greenhouse event venue, with tall black metal framed glass roof, mature green plants, pale stone floor, long oak tables dressed with ivory linen, simple white flowers and candles, elegant natural wood chairs, prepared for an intimate wedding dinner. Entire room and venue architecture visible, no people. Large windows, trees outside, soft warm late afternoon sunlight, natural realistic materials, understated quiet luxury, muted olive, warm beige and creamy white palette, no gold ballroom decor. Wide angle eye-level interior, composition leaves left side quieter and darker for website heading overlay, primary table and attractive plants on the right. No text, no logos, no borders, no watermark. This is an illustration for a fictional venue, do not depict a recognizable real venue.

## Шрифты нового варианта

Cormorant Garamond (500 normal, 500 italic) и Manrope (400, 500, 600, 700) размещены локально в `assets/fonts/`. Лицензии OFL включены в ту же папку. Официальные источники: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond и https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope. При просмотре нет запросов к Google Fonts.
